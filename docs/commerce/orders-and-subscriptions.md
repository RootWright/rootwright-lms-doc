# How to view orders and subscriptions in RootWright LMS

The **Commerce** tab lists every sale on the site. Orders from WooCommerce, Easy Digital Downloads, SureCart, FluentCart and RootWright Checkout are mirrored into one list. Open it from **RootWright LMS → Orders** or **Subscriptions** in the sidebar.

## Orders

![The Orders screen for the current month](/images/commerce/orders-01-list.jpg)

*Commerce → Orders. The list opens on the current month. Remove the month chip to see every order.*

- The summary line reads, for example, "$0 net from 0 orders in November".
- Status tabs show **All**, **Completed**, **Pending**, **Partially refunded**, **Refunded**, **Failed** and **Cancelled**, each with a count and total.
- Search by **Reference, coupon or email**.
- Add filters with the month chip, **+ Store**, **+ Course** and **Needs attention**.
- **Sorted: Date** changes the sort order.
- **Export CSV** downloads the list.
- **Stores** shows the connected stores.

## Subscriptions

::: pro
Subscriptions are part of RootWright LMS Pro.
:::

![The Subscriptions list with no subscriptions](/images/commerce/subscriptions-01-list.jpg)

*Commerce → Subscriptions. A course sold on a recurring plan appears here.*

Filter by status: **Incomplete**, **Trialing**, **Active**, **Past due**, **Cancelled**, **Expired** or **Completed**. Search by **Coupon or gateway subscription ID**.

## Other Commerce screens

- [Discounts](/commerce/discounts) creates coupon codes and automatic discounts.
- [Invoices and transactions](/commerce/invoices-and-transactions) lists invoices, credit notes, payment events and gateway webhooks (Pro).

## Refunds

Refunds are issued from an order. The access rule under [Checkout, tax and access rules](/commerce/checkout-and-tax) decides when access ends.
