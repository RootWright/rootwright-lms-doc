# The course page students see in RootWright LMS

Each published course has a public course page at `/course-catalogue/` followed by the course slug. Visitors use it to decide whether to enroll.

![The public course page](/images/courses/course-page-01-public.jpg)

*The course page for a free course. The price box on the right shows the enroll action.*

## What the page shows

- **Breadcrumbs** back to the course list.
- **Title**, **lesson count**, **number of modules**, **level** and **duration** in a row under the title.
- Three tabs: **Overview** (the description), **Curriculum** (every module and lesson, with the count) and **Instructor**.
- A **price box** with the price (for example **Free**), the lesson count, the duration, the level and the last-updated date.

When a student is enrolled, the price box shows **Continue Learning** in place of the enroll action.

## Students who are logged in

Students who are enrolled see **Continue Learning**, which opens the course player. The player shows the course outline, progress, and each lesson's content. See [What students see](/students/student-experience).

## Notices on the page

Instructors who view their own course see "You are an instructor for this course." in the price box.
