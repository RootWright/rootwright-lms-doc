# How to use course Q&A in RootWright LMS

Course Q&A lets enrolled students ask questions from their dashboard. In RootWright LMS Pro, turn it on for each course in the course builder, then answer questions from **Community → Q&A**.

::: pro
Course Q&A is part of RootWright LMS Pro.
:::

## Turn on Q&A for a course

::: steps
1. Open the course in the course builder and click **Q&A** in the left menu.
2. Under **Who sees questions**, choose a mode. The change saves straight away.
:::

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Off** | Students can't ask questions on this course. The default. |
| **Public** | Every enrolled student sees every question and its answers. |
| **Private** | Each student sees only their own questions, like a support ticket. Questions asked while Private stay private. |

![The Q&A tab in the course builder](/images/community/qa-02-course-tab.jpg)

*Course builder → Q&A. You and permitted teachers answer from the Q&A inbox.*

## What students do

Students ask from their dashboard. They write a **Title** that summarises the question, the **Question** itself, and optionally **Which lesson?** the question is about. Before they post, RootWright LMS may show **These answered questions might help**. Their own questions are under **My questions**.

## Answer questions

::: steps
1. Go to **Community → Q&A**. The **Unanswered** tab lists questions waiting for an answer.
2. Pick a course in **All courses** to narrow the list.
3. Click a question. The thread opens on the right, with a box to post your reply.
4. Write your reply and click **Reply**.
:::

![The Q&A inbox with nothing waiting](/images/community/qa-01-inbox.jpg)

*Community → Q&A. With nothing to answer, it reads "Nothing waiting for an answer. You're all caught up."*

The **All** tab lists every question, answered or not. Replies from you and other teachers are marked **Staff**. To remove a question, open it and delete it.

Students and teachers are emailed about new questions and answers. Edit the emails under **Settings → Emails → Templates**, in the **Course Q&A** group.
